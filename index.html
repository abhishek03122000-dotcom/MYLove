Chapter V

💻
"

I write code for a living. I solve problems methodically. I build systems that have to make logical sense. Every bug has a reason. Every error has a fix. I live in a world that is, by design, predictable.

Then there is you and none of those rules apply anymore.

💓
Something I cannot explain
I have tried to debug this feeling. I cannot. There is no stack trace, no error log, no clear cause. There is just this this constant, warm, running process in the background of everything I do. And I have stopped wanting to fix it.
💓

Chapter VII

The Night She Wrote It Down

📝
"

She had said it before. Many times. Always in that half-joking, half-dreamy way let's just get married, Abhi. I'd laugh. She'd laugh. The moment would pass like warm air. But that night, something shifted in me.

Maybe it was the hour past midnight, the city asleep, neither of us wanting to say goodnight. Maybe it was the way her voice sounded: softer, more sure. She said it again. The same words. But this time, I didn't let it float away.

Puchu 🌸
Let's just get married. We're so good together.
Abhi 💻
Then prove it. Send it to me. In writing. Right now.

I don't know exactly why I asked for proof. Maybe I wanted to see if she really meant it if the casual way she said it matched something deeper. The seconds that followed felt like minutes.

And then my phone buzzed.

💬
Her message sent in less than a minute
Abhishek I'll marry you 2029 🌝

I stared at those words. She didn't hesitate. Didn't ask for time to think. She just wrote it like she had been waiting for me to ask.

That night I didn't reply right away. I sat with it. Let it settle. Fell asleep with my phone still in my hand and that message glowing on the screen. The next morning, I thought about it in detail. What it would mean. What I would have to become. And then I wrote back not a laugh, not a casual "yes" but an honest answer.

🤍
His reply the morning after
2029… Till that day I promise to become the man you'll always be proud to stand beside. The kind of love you can flex to the world. Protect your heart and choose you in every lifetime. ❤️

Her reply was

Puchu 🌸
I already love and adore the man you are 🤝
And I'll be your comfort no matter what. ❤️

No more was needed.

That is the part nobody writes about. Love is not the promise made at midnight. It is what you do the morning after the promise. And every morning after every small fight, we both chose to stay.

🌙
She wrote it first.
I meant it forever.
We fight. We stay.
That is our love.
— Abhi & Puchu
Chapter VIII

✈️
"

Six months of voices. Six months of screens. And somewhere inside all that time, we stopped being two strangers figuring each other out and started becoming a home for each other.

This chapter is about everything that happened while we were waiting to finally stand in the same room. The small intimacies. The big plans. The beautiful mess of loving someone you've never touched yet.

🌧️
🎂

I used to be indifferent about june. It was just a month. Then I learned that june 23rd is your birthday and now june is my favourite month too. I love it because it starts with slow rain. Long night storms. The smell of wet earth outside the window. The sky after the rain clears that pale, washed blue. I started looking up at the sky after rain and thinking of you. You made june beautiful without even trying.
🏠
💬

You had a fight with your roommate. And you told me about it every detail, every frustration. I listened. And somewhere in the middle of it, I felt something shift in me. This wasn't just a phone call. This was what families do. They vent to each other. They trust each other with the small wars of daily life.
